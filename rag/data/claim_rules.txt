AROGYASECURE CLAIM RULES

1. A reimbursement claim must be submitted within 30 days after discharge.
2. Planned cashless hospitalization requires pre-authorization at least 48 hours before admission.
3. Emergency hospitalization should be informed within 24 hours.
4. General hospitalization eligibility requires 6 completed policy months.
5. Maternity eligibility requires 24 completed policy months.
6. Pre-existing disease coverage begins after 36 completed policy months.
7. Accidental hospitalization has no initial waiting period when the policy is active.
8. Original bills and receipts are required for reimbursement claims.
9. Duplicate or altered documents may lead to claim investigation.
10. Cosmetic procedures are excluded unless medically necessary after an accident and approved.
11. Dental treatment is covered only when caused by accidental injury.
12. The insurer may request additional documents for claims above INR 200,000.
13. Missing policy information must never be guessed.
